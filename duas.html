PRAYERS FOR A BEAUTIFUL BEGINNING

Duas & Blessings

❀

We ask Allah to bless this union with love, mercy, peace and lasting barakah.

❀

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَهُمَا وَبَارِكْ عَلَيْهِمَا وَاجْمَعْ بَيْنَهُمَا فِي خَيْرٍ

“O Allah, bless them, bless their union, and bring them together in goodness.”

♡

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

“Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us examples for the righteous.”

Surah Al-Furqan 25:74
✦

A Prayer for Their Journey

May Allah place affection and mercy between their hearts, guide them through every season of life, grant them patience and understanding, and make their home a place of peace.

✿

A Prayer for Their Families

May Allah reward both families for their love and support, keep their hearts united, and fill their homes with health, happiness, protection and barakah.

“May every step they take together bring them closer to Allah and closer to one another.”

❀

Shariq & Eenara

With love, prayers & blessings from our families

Nikkah · Sunday, 1 November 2026 · Karachi
